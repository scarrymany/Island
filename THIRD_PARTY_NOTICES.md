Each font is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; its copyright notice and complete license are installed alongside the application as `licenses/<font filename without .ttf>-OFL.txt`. The corresponding open-source font projects are maintained in the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl). The fonts are embedded in the application and are not installed system-wide.
